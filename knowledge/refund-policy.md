# Refund, Return and Shipping Policy — Aurora Electronics

## Refund window

- You may request a refund within **30 days of delivery**.
- Items must be unused and in their original packaging.
- Items opened beyond inspecting the product are not eligible.

## Refund process

1. Email support@aurora-electronics.example with your order number.
2. We reply with a prepaid return label within 1 business day.
3. Refunds are issued within **5–7 business days** after the returned item
   arrives at our warehouse.
4. Refunds go to the original payment method only.

## Exchanges

- Exchanges are accepted within 30 days for the same item in a different
  color, subject to stock availability.

## Warranty

- All products carry a **12-month limited warranty** covering manufacturing
  defects.
- The warranty does not cover accidental damage, water damage beyond the
  stated IP rating, or normal battery wear.

## Shipping

- Standard shipping: 3–5 business days, $4.99 (free on orders over $50).
- Express shipping: 2 business days, $12.99.
- Orders placed before 2:00 PM CT ship the same day, Monday to Friday.
- Tracking numbers are emailed automatically when the order ships.

## Damaged or wrong items

- Report damaged or incorrect items within 7 days of delivery.
- We cover return shipping in these cases and send a replacement or full
  refund, at your choice.
